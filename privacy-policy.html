Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 September 2026  ·  In effect from 24 September 2026

Wanas is a mental-health support app. Almost everything you tell it is sensitive, and we built it on that assumption rather than adding privacy afterwards. This page explains exactly what we hold, who can see it, and how you get rid of it.

Wanas is not an emergency service. If you are in immediate danger or thinking about harming yourself, contact your local emergency services or a crisis line right away. Messages in Wanas are not monitored in real time.

The short version

  • You can use Wanas under a nickname. Your real name is optional.
  • Your therapist sees only what you have granted them, item by item, and you can withdraw any of it at any moment.
  • Our administrators work with anonymous IDs. They do not routinely see your name, your email, your chats, your journal or your assessment answers.
  • We never sell your data, never use it for advertising, and never run automated diagnosis or risk scoring on it.
  • You can request account deletion from inside the app, or by emailing us.

1. Who we are

Wanas is operated by Omar Marey ("Wanas", "we", "us"), who is the controller of the personal data described below.

For anything about your privacy or this policy, write to wanas@getkensho.ai. If you need a postal address for a formal request, ask and we will provide one.

3. Health data

Much of the above is health data, which in most jurisdictions is a special category needing extra protection. That includes your journal, your assessment answers, the conditions you describe, your therapist's session notes, and your answers to the safety questions.

We process it because you asked us to provide you with a mental-health support service, and, where the law requires it, on the basis of your explicit consent — given when you create an account, and again separately each time you grant a therapist access to a particular category. You can withdraw that access at any time, and withdrawal takes effect immediately for past material as well as future material.

We do not diagnose you and we do not score you. Wanas performs no automated assessment of severity, no clinical risk scoring and no automated decision-making about your care. Everything clinical is a judgement made by a qualified human being.

4. Who can see what

Your therapist

A therapist sees only what your own permission grants allow, and each category is granted separately — your identity, your sensitive profile, your journal and your assessments are four independent decisions, taken per therapist. If you work with two therapists, granting one tells the other nothing.

Revoking a grant removes access immediately, to historical material as well as new material. It is enforced in the database, not by hiding a button in the app.

Our administrators

Administrators keep the service running: approving therapists, handling payments, and dealing with complaints and safety cases. They work with your anonymous ID. They do not ordinarily see your name, your email, your chats, your journal, your assessment answers, your saved safety answers, or your therapist's private notes.

There is one exception. Where a safety or legal process genuinely requires your identity, a named administrator may request it through a separate, authorised procedure. Every such request is written to an audit log that cannot be edited or deleted.

Other members

Other members see only what you present publicly — your display name or nickname, and your participation in community sessions. They never see your profile details, your journal or your conversations.

5. The safety process

If you indicate that you may be at risk, Wanas asks you to confirm before anything else happens. A single answer never, on its own, creates a safety case or alerts anyone. Confirmation comes first, always.

Where a case is opened, your therapist and the authorised safety team can see what is needed to help you — including an emergency contact you have provided, if the situation calls for it.

7. Storage, security and location

  • Every file you upload — photos, documents, voice notes — is held in private storage. Nothing is publicly addressable. Access is granted through short-lived links issued only after we have checked that you are allowed to see that file.
  • Access to every table is enforced at the database level and denied by default, so a bug in the app cannot expose someone else's records.
  • Your chats are encrypted. Privacy comes first. To be precise about what that means: traffic is encrypted in transit and data is encrypted at rest, but this is not end-to-end encryption. We do not claim it is, because our approved safety processes require that the service can act on content when someone is at risk.
  • Your data is stored on Supabase infrastructure in Frankfurt, Germany (EU). If you use Wanas from outside the EU, your data is transferred there with your consent and under the contractual data-protection terms we hold with each provider listed above.

9. Your rights

You can ask us to: give you a copy of your data; correct something wrong; delete your account; restrict or object to how we use your data; or transfer your data elsewhere. You can also withdraw consent at any time — for therapist access you can do this yourself in the app, instantly.

Write to wanas@getkensho.ai and we will reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the data-protection authority where you live. In Egypt this is the Personal Data Protection Centre established under Law No. 151 of 2020.

Deleting your account: our account deletion page explains exactly how it works and what happens afterwards.

10. Children

Wanas is for adults. You must be 18 or over to create an account, and we check your date of birth at sign-up. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has an account, write to wanas@getkensho.ai and we will remove it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that affects you, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top always shows the current version.

نعالجها لأنك طلبت منا تقديم خدمة دعم نفسي لك، واستنادًا — حيثما يشترط القانون ذلك — إلى موافقتك الصريحة التي تمنحها عند إنشاء حسابك، ثم تمنحها مرة أخرى بشكل منفصل كلما منحت معالجًا حق الاطلاع على فئة بعينها. ويمكنك سحب هذا الوصول متى شئت، ويسري السحب فورًا على ما مضى كما على ما هو قادم.

نحن لا نشخّصك ولا نمنحك درجة. لا يجري ونس أي تقييم آلي للشدة، ولا أي حساب آلي للمخاطر، ولا أي قرار آلي بشأن رعايتك. كل ما هو إكلينيكي هو حكم يصدر عن إنسان مؤهل.
